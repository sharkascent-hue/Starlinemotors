Five signs your brakes need attention

Brakes rarely fail without telling you first. These are the signs to watch for, and why it is cheaper to act early.

← All articles

1. Squealing or grinding

A high-pitched squeal usually means the pads are getting low. Grinding means they may be worn through to the metal, which damages the discs as well.

2. A soft or spongy pedal

If the pedal travels further than it used to, or feels soft, have it checked. It can point to a problem in the brake lines or fluid.

3. Pulling to one side

If the car pulls left or right when you brake, one side is doing more work than the other.

4. Vibration through the pedal

A judder through the pedal or steering wheel under braking often points to the discs.

5. A warning light

A brake warning light on the dash should never be ignored.

Don’t neglect your brakes. Brake services at the Car Clinic include brake pad and rotor replacement, brake line repair, and more. Catching worn pads early is far cheaper than replacing pads and discs together.
