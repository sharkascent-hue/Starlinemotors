The Car Clinic · Service

Diagnostic Services

If you’re not sure what’s wrong with your vehicle, bring it in for our diagnostic services. Our experts will diagnose the issue and recommend the necessary repairs.

When to book it in

Signs it needs a look

  • A warning light on the dash
  • A fault you cannot pin down
  • The car going into limp mode
  • An intermittent problem that comes and goes
What we do

How we sort it

  • Computer diagnostics and fault code read
  • Hands-on checks to confirm the cause
  • A plain-English explanation of what we found
  • A recommendation and quote for the repair

One hour bay booking to start. Price varies by vehicle, and we give you a detailed estimate before any work begins.
