About us

More than just a dealership

Proudly located on Greenhills Road. Your trusted partner for all your automotive needs.

Welcome to Starline Motors, proudly located on Greenhills Road, where we are dedicated to being more than just a car dealership. We’re your trusted partner for all your automotive needs. With a reputation for excellence, we offer an extensive range of high-quality vehicles, catering to every lifestyle and budget. Whether you want to buy cars for daily use or family outings, we have you covered.

At Starline Motors, customer satisfaction is our top priority. Our experienced and approachable team is here to guide you through every step of the process, from selecting the perfect vehicle to exploring competitive car financing solutions tailored just for you. We pride ourselves on transparency, ensuring you get clear and honest advice without any unnecessary pressure.

But our commitment doesn’t stop at the sale. We offer comprehensive after-sales support, including servicing, repairs, and maintenance, to keep your vehicle in peak condition. Our modern facilities, equipped with state-of-the-art tools, ensure your car receives the best care possible.

We don’t just sell cars. We create lasting relationships built on trust, quality, and unmatched service. Experience the excitement of a test drive in Dublin and discover why we are Greenhills Road’s favourite car dealership.
