Garage & repairs

How often should I get my oil changed?

We recommend getting your oil changed every 5,000 miles or every six months, whichever comes first. This can help keep your engine running smoothly and prevent damage.

Do I need to schedule an appointment?

While we do accept walk-ins, we highly recommend scheduling an appointment to ensure timely service. You can book an appointment by calling us or through the booking form on this website.

How long will my repair take?

The time required for repairs depends on the complexity of the issue. Simple services like oil changes and tyre rotations can often be completed within an hour, while more complex repairs may take several hours or even a full day. We will provide you with an estimated timeline when you bring your vehicle in.

How much will my repair cost?

Repair costs vary depending on the type of service and the parts required. After diagnosing the issue, we will provide you with a detailed estimate before any work begins. We strive to offer competitive pricing and ensure transparency with no hidden fees.

Are you certified to work on my make and model?

Our technicians are certified and experienced in working on a wide range of vehicle makes and models, including domestic and foreign brands. We undergo regular training to stay up-to-date with the latest automotive technology and repair techniques.

Do you offer emergency or after-hours services?

We understand that car trouble can happen at any time. While we do not offer 24/7 services, we can assist with towing and provide emergency repairs during business hours. For after-hours emergencies, please contact a local towing service to bring your vehicle to our garage, and we will attend to it as soon as we open.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept a variety of payment methods, including cash, major credit cards, and debit cards. If you need to discuss alternative payment options, please let us know in advance.

How can I check the status of my repair?

You can call us at any time during business hours to check the status of your repair. We’ll provide you with updates and let you know when your vehicle is ready for pickup. Additionally, we may offer text or email updates for your convenience.

How do I contact you?

You can reach us by phone, email, WhatsApp or through this website. See the Contact page for our phone numbers, email address, and business hours. We’re always happy to answer any questions or assist with scheduling an appointment.

Buying a car

Do you deliver cars?
